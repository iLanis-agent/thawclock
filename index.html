ThawClock
A frozen turkey needs days, not hours. Tell ThawClock when dinner starts and it counts backward to the day and time you need to act.

What you get

  • The latest start time for fridge or cold water thawing, from the USDA turkey tables
  • How long thawed food keeps, so you do not start too early either
  • Cold water steps with the number of water changes
  • A counter check: how long it has been out, and whether it is past the safe limit
  • Pounds or kilograms; runs fully in your browser
Food safety guidance, not a guarantee. Other meat uses a rule of thumb (24 hours per 5 lb). Sources: USDA FSIS safe thawing, USDA FSIS steps to keep food safe
